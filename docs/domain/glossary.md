# Domain Glossary

These definitions create a shared starting vocabulary; they are not complete
business rules. Unconfirmed details are explicitly TBD.

| Term | Initial meaning | Open questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | A person or organization receiving workshop services or purchasing goods. | Identity, billing, and contact rules are TBD. |
| Vehicle | A vehicle associated with workshop activity and, where applicable, a customer. | Ownership history and unique identifier rules are TBD. |
| Work Order | The operational record used to plan and track requested workshop work for a vehicle. | Lifecycle, approvals, assignment, and pricing rules are TBD. |
| Estimate | A proposed scope and price for work or goods before final billing. | Validity, revision, approval, and conversion rules are TBD. |
| Product / Part | An item that may be purchased, stocked, consumed, or sold. | Catalog, unit, compatibility, and valuation rules are TBD. |
| Stock | The recorded quantity or availability of a product/part at a location. | Reservation, location, and negative-stock rules are TBD. |
| Stock Movement | A record representing a change in stock; detailed semantics are not yet confirmed. | Auditability, movement types and causes, reversals, reservations, and allowed lifecycle behavior are TBD. |
| Invoice | A billing document stating amounts owed for supplied goods or services. | Numbering, tax, status, and legal requirements are TBD. |
| Payment | A record of value received or disbursed and applied to a financial obligation where relevant. | Methods, allocation, refund, and reconciliation rules are TBD. |
